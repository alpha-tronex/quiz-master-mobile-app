# Privacy Policy — Quiz Master

**Last updated: September 14, 2026**

---

## What This App Does

Quiz Master lets you register an account, browse quizzes, take them, and
review your results and quiz history. An account is required to use the app.

---

## Data We Collect

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Name, username, email, phone, address** | Account registration and profile (`/api/register`, `/api/user/update`) | Until you delete your account |
| **Password** | Stored as a hash, never in plain text; used to authenticate you | Until you delete your account |
| **Quiz answers and scores** | To show your results and quiz history (`/api/quiz`, `/api/quiz/history`) | Until you delete your account |
| **Session token** | Keeps you logged in between app launches; stored on-device only, in the OS's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) | Cleared on logout or token expiry |

We do not collect device identifiers, precise location, or advertising
identifiers, and the app does not use analytics or ad SDKs.

---

## How Your Data Is Used

Your account and quiz data is stored on our own servers to run the app's
core features — authentication, quiz delivery, scoring, and history. We do
not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

---

## Data Storage

- Account and quiz data is stored in our database until you delete your
  account.
- Your session token is stored only on your device, not on our servers.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
